Exercise 2 · GrammarToday's grammar

was or were?

Write was, were, wasn't or weren't.

Past Simple: was / were

The past of am / is / are. Like the present, it needs no did.

Positive

I / he / she / it was · you / we / they were

Where you were, how you felt, what something was like.

I was at home last Saturday.

The film was boring.

Negatives and questions

wasn't · weren't · Was she …? · Were you …?

Add not, or put was / were before the subject. No did!

We weren't at school on Monday.

Were you tired? — Yes, I was.

Watch out

We was at home.We were at home.
Did you be tired?Were you tired?
Yesterday I am tired.Yesterday I was tired.

Past Simple: regular and irregular verbs

Finished actions in the past: yesterday, last weekend, two days ago, in 2023.

Regular verbs

V + -ed (the same for all persons)

work → worked · stop → stopped · study → studied · like → liked

I tidied my room on Sunday.

Irregular verbs

go → went · have → had · see → saw · do → did

Learn them by heart — the most common verbs are irregular.

We went to the cinema last night.

Negatives and questions

didn't + V · Did + subject + V?

After did the verb goes back to its base form: no -ed, no went.

She didn't go to the party.

Did you see the film? — Yes, I did.

Watch out

I goed home.I went home.
She didn't went.She didn't go.

The rule in one line

The past is already in did — so the main verb goes back to its base form: Did you see…? · She didn't go. ✗ Did you saw…? · She didn't went.

Exercise 8 · Reading

Nastia's big Saturday

Remember Nastia from Lesson 3? Here is what happened at her competition.

Hi, it's Nastia again! Do you remember my drawing competition? It was last Saturday. I got up at five — it was dark and cold! My dad took me to the station, and the train left at 6:40 exactly. I was very nervous, so I didn't eat anything on the train.

The competition started at ten. There were sixty young artists from all over Ukraine. We had three hours to draw a picture on the theme «My town». I drew the old bridge near my house. I didn't win first prize, but I got third place and a set of professional pencils!

On Sunday I stayed in and had a lie-in until eleven. Now I'm sitting at my desk again and I'm planning my next picture. I usually draw every evening, but today I'm taking a break.

True or false?

  • The competition was on Sunday.
  • Nastia got up very early.
  • She ate breakfast on the train.
  • There were sixty artists at the competition.
  • She won first prize.
  • On Sunday she went out with friends.

Past Simple — минулий час

Ти вже знаєш два теперішні часи. Сьогодні третій час — Past Simple: що сталося і закінчилося в минулому. Це найчастіший минулий час у текстах НМТ.

01 · коли

Завершена дія в минулому

Коли: дія сталася в минулому і вже закінчилась. Зазвичай відомо, коли.

yesterdaylast weekend / week / yeartwo days agoin 2023on Saturday (минулої)when I was a child
ГоловнеСлово ago = «тому»: two days ago — два дні тому. Ставиться після часу.
02 · to be

was / were

+ I / he / she / it was · you / we / they were
− wasn't · weren't
? Was she at home? · Were you tired? — Yes, I was. / No, they weren't.
Як і в теперішньомуЗ to be не потрібне did: Were you tired? ✗ Did you be tired?
03 · правильні дієслова

Правильні: + -ed

Однаково для всіх осіб: I worked, she worked, they worked — ніякого -s!

дієсловощо робимоприклади
більшість+ -edwork → worked · play → played · visit → visited
закінчується на -e+ -dlike → liked · dance → danced
приголосна + yy → -iedstudy → studied · tidy → tidied
короткі: голосна + приголоснаподвоюємоstop → stopped · chat → chatted

Правила ті самі, що з -ing із Lesson 2 — тільки з -ed.
