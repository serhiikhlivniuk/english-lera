Exercise 3 · Grammar

am, is or are?

Choose the right form. In 9–12 write the form yourself — short forms are fine.

The verb to be

am, is and are — the most frequent verb in English. It does not need do/does.

Positive

I am (I'm) · he / she / it is ('s) · you / we / they are ('re)

Age, name, jobs, feelings, places, descriptions.

I'm sixteen.

My mum is a doctor.

Negative

I'm not · he isn't · we aren't

Add not after the verb. No do / does here.

She isn't at home.

We aren't late.

Questions

Am I …? · Is he …? · Are you …? — Yes, I am. / No, he isn't.

Put the verb before the subject.

Are you tired?

Is English difficult?

Watch out

I have sixteen years.I'm sixteen.
She not at home.She isn't at home.
Do you tired?Are you tired?

Exercise 4 · Grammar

he, she, it + -s

Write the form for he / she / it. Careful with -es and -ies.

he / she / it: -s, -es, -ies

In the Present Simple, he, she and it add an ending to the verb. Here is which one.

Most verbs: -s

work → works · play → plays · like → likes

The normal case.

She works in a shop.

-ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: -es

watch → watches · wash → washes · fix → fixes · go → goes · do → does

After these endings we add -es (it is easier to say).

He watches series every evening.

consonant + y: -ies

study → studies · try → tries BUT play → plays

Only when a consonant comes before y. Vowel + y just adds -s.

My sister studies at university.

have → has

I have · she has

The one irregular verb you need now.

He has a dog.

Watch out

She watchs TV.She watches TV.
He studys English.He studies English.
She haves a cat.She has a cat.

Habits, timetables, facts and things that are generally true.

Form

I / you / we / they + V · he / she / it + V-s

Present Simple: questions and short answers

Do and does make questions. After them the verb has no ending.

Yes / No questions

Do + I / you / we / they + V? · Does + he / she / it + V?

The -s moves from the verb to does.

Adverbs of frequency

Where to put always, usually, often and never — and why that position is not random.

The main rule

before the main verb, but AFTER the verb to be

always · usually · often · sometimes · rarely · never

I always do my homework.

He is never late.

With an auxiliary verb

auxiliary + adverb + main verb

When there is can, will, have, or am/is/are + -ing.

She can usually help me.

Frequency expressions

once a week · twice a month · every day — at the start or end of the sentence

These expressions never go in the middle of the sentence.

We train twice a week.

Watch out

Exercise 9 · Reading

Oksana's week

Hi! My name is Oksana. I am sixteen and I live in Poltava with my mum, my dad and my little brother, Maksym. I am in the eleventh grade, and this year is very important for me because I want to pass the NMT.

On weekdays I get up at half past six. I have a quick breakfast — usually toast and tea — and I leave home at half past seven. School finishes at two. After school I have lunch, and then I do my homework. My brother doesn't like homework, so he often hangs out with his friends instead!

I have English lessons with a tutor twice a week, on Tuesdays and Fridays. In my free time I watch series in English with subtitles. I rarely go to bed before eleven, but I always fall asleep very quickly.

True or false?

  • Oksana lives with her grandparents.
  • She gets up at 6:30 on weekdays.
  • She leaves home at eight o'clock.
  • Her brother likes homework.
  • She has English lessons two times a week.
  • She usually goes to bed early.
Grammar hunt

Find three verbs with -s / -es in the text. Who are they about? Then find two adverbs of frequency — where do they stand in the sentence?

How to do this kind of task

1) Read the whole text first, without choosing. 2) For each gap, look at the subject (who?) and the time words (every morning, at the weekend). 3) Cross out the options that are impossible, then choose. In the NMT there is no penalty for a wrong answer — never leave a gap empty.

Граматика уроку

Сьогодні — фундамент. to be і Present Simple трапляються майже в кожному тексті НМТ, і більшість помилок на тесті — саме тут: загублене -s, зайве am / is, неправильне do / does.

01 · основа

Дієслово to be — am / is / are

Коли: хто ти, скільки тобі років, де ти, який ти. Українською ми це дієслово часто пропускаємо («Я студентка»), англійською — ніколи.

+ I am (I'm) · he / she / it is ('s) · you / we / they are ('re)
− I 'm not · she isn't · we aren't
? Am I …? · Is she …? · Are you …? — Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
Пастка для українців✗ I have 16 years. → ✓ I'm 16. Вік — через to be, не через have.
02 · теперішній

Present Simple — стверджувальні речення

Коли: звички, розпорядок дня, розклад, факти, те, що правда взагалі.

I / you / we / they work
he / she / it works
every dayon Mondaysat weekendstwice a weekalwaysusuallynever
Головне правило урокуПісля he / she / it (а також my mum, Oksana, the café, my cat) дієслово отримує -s. Усе.
03 · правопис

-s, -es чи -ies?

закінчення дієсловащо додаємоприклади
більшість-swork → works · like → likes · play → plays
-s, -ss, -sh, -ch, -x, -o-eswatch → watches · wash → washes · fix → fixes · go → goes · do → does
приголосна + yy → -iesstudy → studies · try → tries
голосна + y-splay → plays · buy → buys
have—have → has
04 · заперечення

don't / doesn't

I / you / we / they don't (do not) + work
he / she / it doesn't (does not) + work

Коли є doesn't, дієслово втрачає -s — воно «переїхало» в does.

✗ She doesn't likes tea.
✗ She not like tea.
✓ She doesn't like tea.

08 · НМТ

Як це буде на НМТ

У частині Use of English тобі дають текст із пропусками і чотири варіанти відповіді (A, B, C, D) — як у вправі 10. Часто правильну відповідь підказують підмет (she → -s / does) і слова-маркери часу (every morning → Present Simple).
