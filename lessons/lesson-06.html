Exercise 3 · GrammarToday's grammar

Comparatives: + -er or more?

Write the comparative. Spelling rules are the same as for -ed: big → bigger, happy → happier.

Comparatives: taller, more interesting

Compare two things: adjective + -er, or more + adjective, and then than.

Short adjectives

adjective + -er + than

One syllable, and two syllables ending in -y (y → i).

My brother is taller than me.

English is easier than maths.

Long adjectives

more + adjective + than

Two or more syllables (not ending in -y).

This film is more interesting than the book.

Irregular

good → better · bad → worse · far → further

Learn them by heart.

My English is better than last year.

as … as

as + adjective + as · not as + adjective + as

The same — or not the same.

My room is as big as yours.

The bus isn't as fast as the train.

Exercise 4 · Grammar

Superlatives: the + -est or the most?

Write the superlative with the.

Superlatives: the tallest, the most interesting

The number one in a group: the + adjective + -est, or the most + adjective.

Short adjectives

the + adjective + -est

The same spelling rules as -er: big → the biggest, happy → the happiest.

Kyiv is the biggest city in Ukraine.

Long adjectives

the most + adjective

Two or more syllables (not ending in -y).

Lviv is one of the most beautiful cities in Ukraine.

Irregular

good → the best · bad → the worst · far → the furthest

Learn them by heart.

It was the best day of my life.

in / of

the … in + a place / a group · the … of + a number or time

the tallest in the class · the best of the three · the coldest day of the year

She is the youngest in our family.

Порівняння: bigger, the biggest

Як сказати «більший» і «найбільший»? Є два способи — і все залежить від довжини прикметника. На НМТ ця тема трапляється майже в кожному тесті.

1Короткі

tall → taller → the tallest

1 склад або 2 склади на -y (happy, easy, busy).

Kyiv is bigger than Lviv.

2Довгі

interesting → more interesting → the most interesting

2+ склади (не на -y): modern, expensive, beautiful.

Lviv is more beautiful than…

!Неправильні

good → better → the best
bad → worse → the worst
far → further → the furthest

Вчимо напамʼять, як неправильні дієслова.

01 · вищий ступінь

Comparative — порівнюємо двох

short: adjective + -er + than My brother is taller than me.
long: more + adjective + than This film is more interesting than the book.

than = «ніж, за». Не плутай з then = «потім».

Щоб підсилити: much / a lot / a bit + comparative: Kyiv is much bigger than my village. Today is a bit warmer.

02 · найвищий ступінь

Superlative — номер один у групі

short: the + adjective + -est Kyiv is the biggest city in Ukraine.
long: the most + adjective It's the most beautiful place I know.

Після superlative: in + місце чи група (the tallest in the class), of + число чи час (the best of the three, the coldest day of the year).

03 · правопис

-er / -est: ті самі правила, що для -ed

прикметникщо робимоприклади
більшість+ -er / -estcheap → cheaper → the cheapest
на -e+ -r / -stlarge → larger → the largest · nice → nicer
приголосна + yy → -ier / -iesthappy → happier · busy → the busiest
короткі: голосна + приголоснаподвоюємоbig → bigger · hot → the hottest · thin → thinner
04 · as … as

Однакові — і не однакові

as + adj + as = такий самий
My room is as big as yours.
not as + adj + as = не такий
The bus isn't as fast as the train.
= The train is faster than the bus.
