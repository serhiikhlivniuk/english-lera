Add -ing

Write the -ing form. Careful: some verbs lose -e, some double the last letter.

Spelling: -ing

Three small rules for adding -ing.

Most verbs

V + -ing

Just add -ing.

play → playing, read → reading

Silent -e

-e → -ing

Drop the e at the end.

make → making, write → writing, dance → dancing

Short verbs: vowel + consonant

double the last letter

One vowel + one consonant at the end of a short verb.

swim → swimming, sit → sitting, get → getting, run → running

-ie

-ie → -ying

A small group of verbs.

lie → lying, die → dying

Exercise 4 · GrammarToday's grammar

What is happening right now?

Put the verb in the Present Continuous: am / is / are + -ing.

Present Continuous

Things happening right now, around now, or only for a short time.

Form

I am + V-ing · he / she / it is + V-ing · you / we / they are + V-ing

Both parts are needed: am / is / are AND the -ing verb.

I'm doing my homework now.

My mum is cooking dinner.

Negatives and questions

I'm not · she isn't · they aren't + V-ing · Am / Is / Are + subject + V-ing?

No do / does here — to be does all the work.

He isn't listening.

Are you watching TV? — No, I'm not.

Time words

now · right now · at the moment · today · this week · Look! · Listen!

These words usually tell you to choose the Present Continuous.

Look! It's snowing.

This week she is staying with her grandma.

Watch out

She cooking now.She is cooking now.
I am work now.I am working now.
Do you listening?Are you listening?

Exercise 6 · Grammar

Every day or right now?

Choose the Present Simple or the Present Continuous. Look for the time words first.

Present Simple or Present Continuous?

The most common choice in NMT grammar tasks: a habit or something happening now?

Present Simple = always / usually

habits · routines · facts · timetables

Time words: every day, usually, often, never, on Mondays.

My dad usually drinks tea.

She works in a bank.

Present Continuous = now / for a short time

now · at the moment · today · this week · Look!

Something in progress, or a temporary situation.

Today he is drinking coffee.

She is working from home this week.

State verbs: no -ing

like · love · hate · want · know · understand · need · believe · have (= own)

These verbs describe feelings, thoughts and possession, not actions — use the Present Simple even with now.

I know the answer now.

She wants a new phone.

Watch out

I am knowing him.I know him.
Look! He plays football.Look! He is playing football.
She is going to the gym every day.She goes to the gym every day.

Exercise 7 · Grammar

Verbs that don't like -ing

Choose the correct form. One of them is a trap!

likelovehatewantneedknowunderstandhave (= own)

1. I ___ the answer!

2. My sister ___ chocolate.

3. ___ me?

4. My brother ___ a new phone.

5. They ___ a big dog.

6. I ___ dinner now — can I call you later?

The trap in number 6

have = own (I have a dog) → no -ing. But have = eat / take (have dinner, have a shower, have a nap) is an action → I'm having dinner now.

Граматика уроку

Сьогодні другий теперішній час — Present Continuous. Українською «я читаю» — це і «щодня», і «зараз». Англійською це два різні часи, і вибір між ними — одне з найчастіших завдань у Use of English на НМТ.

01 · форма

Present Continuous — am / is / are + -ing

Коли: те, що відбувається прямо зараз, або тимчасово — «цього тижня», «цього місяця».

+ I am reading · he / she / it is reading · you / we / they are reading
− I'm not reading · she isn't reading · they aren't reading
? Am I …-ing? · Is she reading? · Are you reading? — Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
nowright nowat the momenttodaythis weekthis monthLook!Listen!
Головне правило урокуПотрібні обидві частини: am / is / are і дієслово з -ing. ✗ She reading. ✗ She is read. → ✓ She is reading.
02 · правопис

Як додати -ing

дієсловощо робимоприклади
більшість+ -ingplay → playing · read → reading · study → studying
закінчується на німе -e-e прибираємоmake → making · write → writing · dance → dancing
короткі: голосна + приголоснаподвоюємо останню літеруswim → swimming · sit → sitting · run → running · get → getting
-ie-ie → -yinglie → lying · die → dying
03 · вибір часу

Present Simple чи Present Continuous?

Present Simple — завжди, зазвичай, факт
every day · usually · often · never · on Mondays
My dad usually drinks tea.
Present Continuous — зараз, тимчасово
now · at the moment · today · this week · Look!
Today he is drinking coffee.
ПорадаСпочатку шукай слово-маркер. Немає маркера — спитай себе: «це звичка чи це відбувається саме зараз?»
04 · пастка

Дієслова, які не люблять -ing

Дієслова почуттів, думок і володіння — know, understand, like, love, hate, want, need, believe, have (= мати) — стоять у Present Simple, навіть коли йдеться про «зараз».

✗ I am knowing the answer.
✗ She is wanting a new phone.
✓ I know the answer.
✓ She wants a new phone.
Винятокhave у значенні «їсти, приймати»: have dinner, have a shower, have a nap — це дія, тому I'm having dinner now.

07 · НМТ

Як це буде на НМТ

У Use of English у тексті поруч стоять звичка і «зараз», як у вправі 9: Marta usually plays basketball, but this month she is preparing… Слова but, this month, at the moment — сигнал, що час змінюється.
